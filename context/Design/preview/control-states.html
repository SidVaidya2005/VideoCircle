Engaged is a white fill, never red. Several toggles can be engaged at once in a call; if "on" were red, red would stop meaning danger exactly when Leave needs it most. Red appears twice only: your own muted mic, and Leave. A stopped camera is neutral — the slashed icon carries it.
Press does not shrink. The kit inverts the fill instead, and keyboard focus reuses that same inverted state — so focus and press look identical by design.
Transitions are asymmetric — 50ms ease-out in, 250ms ease-in-out out. CSS only: this bar lives inside <LiveKitRoom>, where JS animation competes with video encoding. Every control carries an aria-label, and every toggle an aria-pressed.
An unavailable capability is absent, not disabled. Screen share renders only where navigator.mediaDevices.getDisplayMedia exists. It exists on no mobile browser — not iOS Safari, not Chrome, Firefox, Samsung Internet, or Opera on Android — so on a phone the control simply is not there. A permanently dead button that can never be enabled on this device is noise, and it invites a tap that can only disappoint.
The full bar does not fit a phone, and hit areas do not shrink. Seven controls at the 44px floor plus the wider Leave measure 440px — wider than a 430px iPhone, let alone a 360px Android. Below sm: (640px) only mic, camera, MORE, and Leave stay on the bar; chat, participants, screen share, and raise hand move into the MORE sheet. Mic, camera, and Leave are never collapsed — they are the controls someone reaches for under pressure.
