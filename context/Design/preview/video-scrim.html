Any text over live video rides a scrim. Every other contrast pairing in this kit assumes a known --bg-*; a video tile has no known background, and the worst case is a bright window behind the participant.
Use --scrim-tile (bottom-up gradient, --scrim-tile-height) for tile labels, --scrim-flat for a full overlay. Label text is --fg-1 on the scrim, never --fg-3 — muted greys are for known backgrounds only.
No backdrop-blur. The brand uses flat semi-opaque fills; glassmorphism is not part of it, and the blur costs GPU on exactly the phones the call is hardest on.
